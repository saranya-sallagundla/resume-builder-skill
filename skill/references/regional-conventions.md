# Regional Conventions

Infer the market from the JD (company location, currency, spelling, phone format) or ask once. Apply on top of the core standards; where they conflict, the market wins.

| Market | Length norm | Photo | Personal details | Terminology & notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **India** | 2 pages (experienced), 1 page (fresher) | No | Phone (+91), email, city. No DOB, marital status, father's name, passport no., or "Declaration" line — all outdated and hurt with MNCs. | "Resume" or "CV" both fine. Notice period and current/expected CTC belong in the application form or email, not the resume, unless the JD asks. Indian numbering (lakh/crore) only if the JD uses it; otherwise use K/M. |
| **United States** | 1 page < 10 yrs, 2 pages otherwise | Never | City, State only. No DOB, photo, nationality, marital status (anti-discrimination law). | "Resume". US spelling. Dates as "Jan 2023 – Present". Work authorization only if asked. No "References available on request". |
| **United Kingdom** | 2 pages | No | City, phone, email. No DOB, nationality, marital status. | "CV". UK spelling (organise, programme). Personal profile = summary. Right-to-work status only if requested. |
| **EU (Germany, Netherlands, Nordics, France)** | 2 pages | Germany/France: photo still common but optional; Netherlands/Nordics: rare | Germany: DOB and nationality sometimes expected; elsewhere omit. | "CV" / "Lebenslauf". Europass format not required for private sector. Language proficiency (CEFR levels: B2, C1) always listed. |
| **Gulf (UAE, KSA, Qatar)** | 2 pages | Often expected | Nationality, visa status, DOB, languages commonly included; driving licence for field roles. | "CV". State current location and availability to relocate. |
| **Australia / NZ** | 2–3 pages acceptable | No | City, phone, email. No DOB or photo. | "Resume" (AU) / "CV" (NZ). Visa/work-rights status expected. Referees section common — "available on request" is acceptable here. |
| **Singapore / SEA** | 2 pages | Optional, declining | Nationality and work-pass status usually expected. | "Resume". Expected salary often requested in application — keep off the resume unless asked. |
| **Canada** | 1–2 pages | Never | City, Province. No DOB, photo, SIN, marital status. | "Resume". Canadian/UK spelling. Work-permit status only if asked. |

## Universal regardless of market

- Standard section headers, single column, no tables/graphics — ATS platforms are global.
- Reverse-chronological.
- Phone in international format when applying across borders.
- LinkedIn URL as plain text.
- Check the JD's spelling (optimise/optimize, programme/program) and match it.
