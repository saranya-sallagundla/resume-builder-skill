# The Complete Powerful Resume Standard
A self-contained checklist of everything a resume must have to pass ATS screening, survive the recruiter's 6–10 second scan, and get shortlisted for interviews. Reusable for any role, any JD.

---

## 1. Document & Formatting Standards

- **Length:** Exactly 2 pages for 5+ years of experience; 1 page for under 5 years. Never 1.5 pages — fill or trim.
- **Margins:** Narrow (0.5"–0.75") on all sides. No large blank areas or trailing white space.
- **Font:** Calibri, Arial, or Georgia only. Body 10.5–11.5 pt; name 16–18 pt; section headers 12–13 pt bold.
- **File format:** .docx or text-based PDF. Never image-based/scanned PDF. Filename: `Firstname_Lastname_Role.docx`.
- **Layout:** Single column. No tables, text boxes, columns, icons, graphics, photos, charts, or skill-rating bars.
- **Headers/footers:** Nothing important inside them — many ATS parsers skip them entirely. Contact info goes in the body.
- **Line spacing:** 1.0–1.15; consistent spacing before/after every section; no orphan lines at page breaks.
- **Order:** Reverse-chronological, always.
- **Consistency:** One date format throughout (e.g., "Jan 2023 – Present"), identical bullet symbols, identical punctuation (all bullets end with period, or none do), consistent tense (current role = present tense, past roles = past tense).

## 2. Required Sections (in this order)

1. **Header:** Name, phone, professional email, city + country, LinkedIn URL. No full street address, no photo, no date of birth.
2. **Headline (one line under name):** Target job title + years of experience + top 2–3 keywords/certifications. Example: *Senior Mainframe Technical Lead | 14+ Years | z/OS, COBOL, DB2 | ITIL v4*.
3. **Professional Summary:** 3–4 lines. Target title, years, domain, 2–3 signature achievements with numbers, top JD keywords baked in. No first-person pronouns, no objectives ("seeking a role...").
4. **Skills:** Clean grouped list (e.g., Monitoring Tools / ITSM / Platforms / Methodologies). Exact JD terms verbatim where truthful. Spell out each acronym once: "Change Advisory Board (CAB)".
5. **Work Experience:** Company, title, location, dates, then bullets. One line of context per company if not well-known ("US healthcare payer, 40K employees").
6. **Certifications:** Explicit names with issuing body and year. In-progress ones as "In Progress – Expected Mon YYYY".
7. **Education:** Degree, institution, year. No GPA unless exceptional and recent. No high-school details for experienced candidates.
8. Optional (only if they earn their space): Awards, Publications, Key Projects.

Use only these standard header names: Summary, Skills, Work Experience, Certifications, Education. Never "My Journey," "What I Bring," etc.

## 3. ATS Keyword Optimization

- **Keyword mirroring:** Extract 15–20 exact phrases from the JD — tool names, certifications, titles, methodologies — and use them verbatim where truthful. ATS does literal/semantic matching.
- **Top-third weighting:** Summary + Skills + most recent role should together contain ~80% of the JD's must-have keywords. Many ATS weight the top third of page 1 more heavily.
- **Both forms of acronyms:** "Information Technology Infrastructure Library (ITIL)" — matches whichever form the recruiter searches.
- **Title alignment:** If your official title differs from the JD's language, align where truthful — e.g., "Systems Engineer (Incident Management Lead)" — or ensure the JD's phrase appears in your bullets.
- **No keyword stuffing:** Every keyword must live inside a truthful sentence or the skills list. White-text keywords or repetition get resumes rejected by humans even when ATS passes them.
- **One resume per JD:** Retailor keywords for every application. Generic resumes score 40–60%; tailored ones score 80%+.

## 4. Bullet Writing Standards (the human scan)

- **XYZ formula:** "Accomplished X, as measured by Y, by doing Z." Numbers up front: *Reduced P1 resolution time 30% by implementing an automated escalation matrix, cutting MTTR across 200+ monthly tickets.*
- **Strong verbs only:** Led, automated, reduced, resolved, delivered, migrated, negotiated, designed. Never "responsible for," "helped with," "worked on," "involved in," "assisted."
- **Quantify scope, not just results:** team size, ticket volume, servers/apps supported, users impacted, uptime %, SLA %, budget, regions, environments. Scope tells the recruiter your level.
- **Bullet count by recency:** Most recent role 5–7 bullets; previous role 4–5; older roles 2–3 lines. Last two roles get 60–70% of total space.
- **Length:** 1–2 lines per bullet, never 3. One idea per bullet.
- **Business impact at senior level:** cost savings, risk reduction, audit/compliance outcomes, stakeholder and vendor management — not only technical tasks.
- **Zero passive voice, zero typos.** Recruiters treat writing quality as a proxy for work quality.

## 5. Numbers & Metrics That Must Appear

A powerful resume has a number in at least 60–70% of bullets. Categories to mine:
- **Money:** cost saved, revenue enabled, budget managed, penalty avoided
- **Time:** resolution time cut, delivery accelerated, downtime reduced, hours automated away
- **Volume:** tickets/month, incidents handled, servers/apps/users supported, transactions processed
- **Quality:** SLA %, uptime % (99.9%), first-call resolution %, defect/error reduction %, CSAT
- **Scale:** team size led, projects run in parallel, sites/regions covered, migration size
- **Growth:** promotions, expanded scope, ownership increases across roles

If exact numbers are unavailable, use honest ranges ("200+", "~15%") — never invent.

## 6. Narrative & Positioning Standards

- **Progression story:** Across roles, the reader should see growing scope, ownership, and impact. Bigger numbers over time.
- **3-second header test:** Name → target title → years → top certification, all visible without scrolling.
- **Recency rule:** Anything older than 10–12 years is compressed or summarized under "Earlier Experience."
- **Ruthless relevance:** Cut every line that doesn't map to the target JD. On 2 pages, every line must earn its spot.
- **Red flags handled:** Gaps >6 months, title downgrades, or short stints either explained briefly (contract role, project completion, sabbatical) or restructured so they don't jump out.
- **No age signals:** No graduation years older than ~15 years, no "25 years of experience" (cap at "15+"), no outdated tech unless the JD asks for it.

## 7. Humanization Standards (must not read AI-written)

- Vary sentence rhythm and structure; never start 3 consecutive bullets with the same verb.
- Use domain-specific phrasing only a real practitioner would use.
- Ban AI clichés: spearheaded, leveraged, synergy, seamlessly, cutting-edge, passionate, results-driven, dynamic, proven track record.
- Include 1–2 specifics that only the candidate would know (a named migration, a specific tool version, a real audit).
- Read the summary aloud — if it sounds like a template, rewrite it.

## 8. Honesty & Risk Standards

- Never fabricate titles, dates, employers, certifications, or metrics — background checks kill offers.
- Keywords may only be added where the underlying experience is real.
- Certifications listed must be verifiable; in-progress ones labeled as such.
- Estimated numbers phrased honestly ("~", "200+") rather than falsely precise.

## 9. Pre-Submission Checklist

- [ ] ATS test: copy-paste the resume into a plain text editor — does everything survive in the right order?
- [ ] Keyword check: top 15 JD keywords present, most within the top third?
- [ ] Number check: 60%+ of bullets quantified?
- [ ] Verb check: no weak/banned verbs, no verb repeated back-to-back?
- [ ] Scan test: can a stranger state your target role, level, and top skill in 6 seconds?
- [ ] Format check: dates consistent, tense consistent, punctuation consistent, exactly 2 full pages?
- [ ] Zero typos (spell-check + one human read-aloud pass)
- [ ] File named properly and saved as .docx / text-PDF
- [ ] LinkedIn headline and skills mirror the resume (recruiters cross-check)

## 10. Process Standards (outside the document)

- Apply within 48–72 hours of posting — application order affects visibility in ATS queues.
- One tailored resume per JD; keep a "master resume" with everything, then cut down per application.
- Referral note: 2–3 tailored lines when applying through a contact.
- Track applications (JD saved, resume version used, date) so interview prep matches what was submitted.
