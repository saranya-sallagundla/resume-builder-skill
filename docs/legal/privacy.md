# Privacy

Short version: **Vriddhih has no server and no accounts, so it has nothing to store about you.**

## What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no cookies set by this site.

## Where your data lives

Everything you type or paste is processed in your browser. If you choose to save items — questions you rated, a tracker board, a brag doc — they are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. You can export them as a file (optionally encrypted with a passphrase you choose) and delete everything with one click. Clearing your browser data also removes them.

## Share links

A share link encodes only the score numbers in the address. It never contains your resume text or personal details.

## The optional AI mode

AI is off by default. If you turn it on:

- **On-device mode** runs a model inside your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. The model files are downloaded once from the runtime's public host and cached by your browser.
- **Your-own-key mode** sends the text you choose to the AI provider you selected, directly from your browser, using an API key that you own. The key is kept only in your browser's storage, never in the address bar, never in this project. That provider's privacy terms apply to what you send — free tiers may use submitted text to improve their services. A "strip personal details" option removes emails, phone numbers and addresses before sending. You can forget the key at any time.

## Third parties we cannot avoid

- **Hosting.** The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.
- **Fonts.** Typefaces load from Google Fonts. Google may log the request under its own privacy policy. If you prefer, your browser's system font is used when the download is blocked.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 16.

## Changes

This page changes when the site's behaviour changes. The date of the last change is in the repository history.
