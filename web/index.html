    Disclaimer

    Vriddhih is a free, open-source tool made to help people with their careers. Please read this before relying on it.

    What it is

    • A set of rule-based helpers that run in your browser: résumé scoring and examples, interview questions, salary ranges, checklists and scripts.
    • An optional AI mode that you switch on yourself, using either a model running on your own device or an API key you own.

    What it is not

    • Not professional advice. Nothing here is legal, financial, tax, immigration or employment advice. For decisions about contracts, notice periods, taxes, visas or disputes, talk to a qualified professional in your country.
    • Not a guarantee. Scores estimate how typical screening software and readers behave. Real systems differ, and hiring decisions depend on many things this tool cannot see.
    • Not your facts. Generated résumés, bullets and stories are labelled example. They show shape and wording. Every number and claim you submit to an employer must be your own and must be true.
    • Not a salary authority. Ranges are estimates built from cited public sources on a stated date, from your own inputs, or both. Markets move. Confirm with at least two other sources before negotiating.
    • Not always right, when AI is on. Language models make mistakes and can invent details. Check everything they produce.

    Privacy

    Short version: Vriddhih has no server and no accounts, so it has nothing to store about you.

    What we collect

    Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no cookies set by this site.

    Where your data lives

    Everything you type or paste is processed in your browser. If you choose to save items — questions you rated, a tracker board, a brag doc — they are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. You can export them as a file (optionally encrypted with a passphrase you choose) and delete everything with one click. Clearing your browser data also removes them.

    Share links

    A share link encodes only the score numbers in the address. It never contains your résumé text or personal details.

    The optional AI mode

    AI is off by default. If you turn it on:

    • On-device mode runs a model inside your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. The model files are downloaded once from the runtime's public host and cached by your browser.
    • Your-own-key mode sends the text you choose to the AI provider you selected, directly from your browser, using an API key that you own. The key is kept only in your browser's storage, never in the address bar, never in this project. That provider's privacy terms apply to what you send — free tiers may use submitted text to improve their services. A "strip personal details" option removes emails, phone numbers and addresses before sending. You can forget the key at any time.

    Third parties we cannot avoid

    • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.
    • Fonts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts. Google may log the request under its own privacy policy. If the download is blocked, your system font is used.
